Ugian Launcher – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

In short: Ugian Launcher does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Your settings are kept only on your device; the app has no internet permission and no data is sent off your device.

1. Data collected

The app does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, messages or any other personal data. It uses no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries.

2. Information stored on your device

Your home screen layout, pages, folders, favorites, hidden apps, widgets, theme, color, language and mode preferences are kept only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are not included in Google backup or device-to-device transfer; they are permanently deleted when you uninstall the app and do not come back if you reinstall it.

In Classic mode, while "Suggested apps" is on, the app counts how many times you open each app from the launcher so that your most used apps appear at the top of the app drawer. These counts are stored only on your device; turning off Settings > Suggested apps stops the counting and deletes the stored counts.

3. List of installed apps

To show them on the home screen and in the app menu, the name and icon of installed apps are read through Android's standard launcher interface (LauncherApps). This list is processed only on your device and is not saved or shared.

To display the taskbar and theme correctly, the battery level, the wallpaper colors and the default assistant setting are also read on the device only; they are not saved or shared.

4. Notification access (optional)

Android's "Notification access" permission can be used to show unread notification dots on app icons. It is requested only if you agree in Settings > Notification dots.

  • Only the number of notifications per app is counted.
  • The title, text and content of notifications are not read or stored.
  • No data leaves your device.

If you don't grant it, the app works normally and only the dots are hidden. You can turn it off at any time in Android settings.

5. Modify system settings permission (Desktop mode)

The brightness control in the taskbar uses Android's "Modify system settings" permission. It is used only to adjust screen brightness and is requested the first time you press the brightness button. No other system setting is changed.

6. Notification panel

In Classic mode, swiping down on the home screen opens the notification panel using Android's "expand status bar" permission. This permission only opens the panel; it does not read notifications.

7. Third-party widgets and apps

Widgets you add to the home screen are run by their own apps and are subject to their own privacy policies. When you open apps such as Google Search or Chrome, those apps' policies apply. Ugian Launcher does not access their data.

Ugian Launcher is sold through Google Play. Payment is processed by Google; we do not have access to your card or payment details.
